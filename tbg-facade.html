TBG Facade — unfolding

A single panel unfolds. From it, more panels unfold, and from those, more — until the drag runs out and the structure settles.

Seed
Unfolding
Drag shapes how a panel decelerates as it opens — 1 is a steady swing, 8 eases to a near-stop as it reaches its angle.
Branch falloff is what decides the final size: each generation is less likely to throw new panels than the last, so the growth tapers out on its own.
Each generation unfolds slower than the last. When it drops below the stall threshold, nothing further unfolds and the structure is finished.
Branching
Straightness biases each branch toward carrying on in the same direction. Low values bush outward, high values run in long ribbons.
Fold back chance decides how often a panel hinges the other way, so the structure grows into the space on both sides of the plane.
Panel style
Loop
With auto-regrow on, each finished structure holds, fades, and a fresh seed begins unfolding — an endless sequence of one-off sculptures.
