Genre shapes what ⚄ Generate produces (and sets BPM/swing) — tracks 1–6 are treated as drums (1 kick, 2 snare/clap, 3 closed hat, 4 open hat, 5–6 perc), 7–9 as one-shot samples, 10 as the loop, 11 as bass. BANK/SND pick which sample/drum voice actually plays (CC24/CC12) — that's what makes track 1 a kick and track 3 a hat, not the note number. NOTE only tunes/transposes what's loaded. TONE matches the front-panel knob (−60..+60) and gets pushed to the unit on connect, so it won't drift from whatever was last dialed in by hand. HOLD sets how long a note stays on before Note Off — leave it blank ("auto") for drum hits to use the Gate slider, or give one-shot/loop/bass tracks a real value (ms) so the sample actually plays out instead of being cut off. Tap a step to cycle off → hit → accent (bass also gets slide, in teal). Tap ▸ to test a track using its current Hold length.
