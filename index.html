Shared parts

Modules and utilities the tools sit on top of.

On the drive layer. unlimiter-drive.js is the shared modulation module: audio band analysis, onset detection, MIDI clock and CC, clock-locked LFOs, and a mod matrix that routes any of it to any parameter. Tools carrying the drive tag have it wired in.

Microphone and MIDI need a secure context. Served from GitHub Pages over https they work. Opened as a file:// URL from your disk they are blocked by the browser. Run drive-check.html first if anything seems dead.
